# Operational LLWAS data access and publication safeguards

Original airport LLWAS measurements and related operational alert records may be subject to data-owner and institutional restrictions. Do **not** upload raw 10-second measurements, one-minute derived wind tables, timestamp-level labels, fold-index manifests tied to real observations, or per-anchor prediction records to a public repository without documented permission.

The provided synthetic example is original fictional data and is not related to real Juanda conditions. Aggregate publication tables are offered only for author review; ensure approval before public release. Model weights also require a permissions and operational-sensitivity review.

Recommended requester process: contact corresponding author and data holder; specify requested data subset, purpose, retention policy, and distribution limitations. No automatic access to operational data is granted by this repository.
